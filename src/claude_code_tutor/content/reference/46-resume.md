# /resume

`/resume [id|name]` returns to a previous conversation with its full history
intact (a picker opens if you don't name one). It's how you continue yesterday's
work instead of starting over. Its close cousins are [/branch](lesson:ref-branch),
which forks without losing the original, and [/rewind](lesson:ref-rewind), which
rolls back within a session — together they let you move freely through your own
history. The opposite move is [/clear](lesson:ref-clear).

**Related:** [/branch](lesson:ref-branch) · [/rewind](lesson:ref-rewind) · [/clear](lesson:ref-clear)
