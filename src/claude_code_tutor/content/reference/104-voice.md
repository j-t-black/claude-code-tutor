# /voice

`/voice` toggles voice dictation — speak your prompts instead of typing, with
hold-to-talk or tap modes. Handy for long, discursive prompts or when your hands
are busy. The mode is configurable in [/config](lesson:ref-config).

**Related:** [/config](lesson:ref-config)
